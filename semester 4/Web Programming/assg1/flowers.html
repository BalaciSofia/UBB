Tulip

Tulips are one of the most popular spring flowers. They are strongly associated with the Netherlands and symbolize love and new beginnings.

You can read more about tulips on Wikipedia.

Popular Types of Tulips

  • Darwin Hybrid Tulips - Large flowers and very resistant.
    More info: Darwin Hybrids
  • Parrot Tulips - Wavy and colorful petals.
    More info: Parrot Tulips
  • Triumph Tulips - Classic tulip shape and many colors.
    More info: Triumph Tulips

Interesting fact: During the 17th century, tulips were so valuable in the Netherlands that they caused an economic event known as Tulip Mania .

Peony

The peony or paeony is any flowering plant in the genus Paeonia, the only genus in the family Paeoniaceae. Peonies are native to Asia, Europe, and Western North America. Scientists differ on the number of species that can be distinguished, ranging from 25 to 40, although the current consensus describes 33 known species.
